Perth Airport Taxi FAQs

Quick answers to the most common airport transfer questions.

How early should I book a Perth Airport taxi?

For early morning flights, book the night before. For daytime flights, 2–3 hours notice is usually enough. During peak holiday weekends, 1–2 days in advance is safer to lock in your preferred pickup time.

Do you cover all Perth Airport terminals?

Yes. We cover T1 (international, plus some domestic), T2, T3 and T4 (Qantas) for both pickups and drop-offs across all airlines.

Do you track flights for arrivals?

Yes. Share your flight number and we monitor arrival times. If your flight is delayed or early, pickup is adjusted automatically.

Can I pre-book a return airport pickup?

Absolutely. Many travellers pre-book both legs at the same time. Share your departure date and flight number for the return and we will plan pickup based on landing time.

Do you provide child seats or maxi vehicles?

Yes. Mention child seats, baby capsules or maxi cab needs at booking and we will dispatch a suitable vehicle.

How much does a Perth Airport taxi cost?

All fares follow standard Perth taxi rates based on distance and time. We can give an upfront estimate before booking. Typical guide: Perth CBD $45–$55, Fremantle $70–$85, Joondalup $65–$80.

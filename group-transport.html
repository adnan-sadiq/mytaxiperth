Maxi Cab & Group Transport FAQs

Quick answers to common group booking questions.

How many passengers fit in a maxi cab?

Standard maxi cabs accommodate 7 to 11 passengers depending on the vehicle. Tell us your group size and luggage and we will send the right one.

Can I book a maxi cab for an airport transfer?

Yes. Maxi cab airport transfers are popular for families and travelling teams. We monitor flights and align pickup time with your departure or arrival.

Do you handle weddings and events?

Yes. Weddings, birthdays, corporate events, sports games, and concerts. We can plan both outbound and return trips so guests are not left stranded.

Can children travel in a maxi cab?

Yes. Tell us how many child seats or booster seats you need and we will arrange them. We support family bookings across all Perth metro suburbs.

How much notice do you need for group bookings?

We accept on-demand bookings, but for large groups (events and airport transfers) we recommend at least 24 hours notice to lock in availability.

Can I split the fare between passengers?

Yes. Group fares can be split easily between passengers. We can also issue a receipt for corporate or shared expense reporting.

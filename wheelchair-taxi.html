Wheelchair Taxi FAQs

Common questions about accessible taxi bookings in Perth.

Do you accept TUSS vouchers?

Yes. We accept Taxi User Subsidy Scheme (TUSS) vouchers for eligible passengers. Please mention you have a voucher when booking.

How much notice do you need for a wheelchair taxi?

We take 24/7 on-demand bookings, but pre-booking is strongly recommended for medical appointments and airport transfers so we can guarantee a suitable vehicle.

Are your drivers trained for wheelchair passengers?

Yes. Our drivers are trained to assist with secure restraints and allow extra boarding time without rushing the passenger.

Can I book recurring wheelchair rides?

Yes. We support regular medical, therapy and day-program bookings. We can save your usual requirements and streamline future trips.

Do you support electric and motorised wheelchairs?

Yes. Our vehicles include ramps and tie-down points designed for both manual and motorised wheelchairs.

Which Perth suburbs do you cover?

All Perth metropolitan suburbs — central, north, south and east. See full suburb list.
